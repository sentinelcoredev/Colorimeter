Measurement Best Practices

  • Consistent Environment: Always photograph indoors under diffuse, indirect ambient light. Avoid direct sunlight, bright window glare, or camera flashes.
  • Fixed Geometry: Keep the camera at an identical distance and angle relative to your skin.
  • Clean Surface: Ensure the skin is clean, dry, and free of sweat, sunscreen, or cosmetics that alter specular reflection.
  • No Filters: Ensure phone beauty modes, AI enhancements, and color filters are strictly disabled.

Scientific Disclaimer

This tool measures relative, camera-dependent visible skin color changes over time using standardized CIELAB color space and CIEDE2000 color difference formulas ($\Delta E_{00}$).

It does NOT measure melanin concentration, pigmentation depth, or absolute tanning percentages. Mobile phone cameras dynamically adjust white balance, exposure, and tone mapping across captures, meaning apparent color shifts can stem from lighting or sensor processing rather than biological pigment changes.

Data Management

All data is stored locally in your browser via LocalStorage. No data is transmitted externally.

How to Use Skin Tan Tracker

  1. Select your target body area (Forearm, Face, etc.) on the Dashboard.
  2. Go to the Capture & ROI tab and open your camera or upload a clear photo.
  3. Select a uniform skin patch on the preview canvas, avoiding hair, moles, or shadows.
  4. Review the extracted $L^*a^*b^*$ values and quality score, add notes, and save.
  5. Track your CIEDE2000 ($\Delta E_{00}$) color difference progression over days on the dashboard chart.
